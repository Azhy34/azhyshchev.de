Engineering Deep Dive

Two-Stage LLM Pipeline & Asynchronous Audio Generation Architecture

Two-tier prompt decomposition for creative lyrics: Generative audio tools like Suno require tightly structured lyrical prompts with verse, chorus, and bridge breakdowns, tagged musical cues, and strict rhyme meters. Standard single-pass prompts often yield either melodically rigid or emotionally generic lyrics. This architecture divides lyric generation into two coordinated LLM stages: Gemini 2.5 Flash acts as a high-speed idea expander, producing four distinct lyrical structures, meter options, and genre hooks in under 500ms. Claude Sonnet then reviews the selected candidate, elevating emotional subtlety, refining syllable stress for vocal cadence, and ensuring pristine genre fit.

Non-blocking asynchronous audio workers: Generative audio synthesis runs between 25 and 65 seconds per track. Holding open synchronous HTTP client connections on mobile browsers leads to client timeouts and dropped requests. The backend leverages an asynchronous job queue backed by PostgreSQL task states and webhook callbacks. When the Suno API completes audio rendering, the asset is automatically mirrored to persistent storage, and the client browser receives an instant notification via SSE (Server-Sent Events) without full-page reloads.

Transactional idempotency & token accounting: Managing paid customer generations requires rock-solid financial safeguards. The system implements an append-only token ledger in PostgreSQL where generation attempts and Shopify order webhooks are cryptographically validated with HMAC signatures. Webhook deduplication guarantees that customers are never charged twice for retry attempts or interrupted streaming sessions.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a full-stack AI SaaS product from scratch?
This AI Music SaaS was built and shipped to production in 2 weeks using AI-assisted engineering workflows. The architecture includes a Next.js 14 frontend, Python API backend, dual-LLM lyric processing (Gemini 2.5 Flash + Claude Sonnet), Suno V5.5 music generation, Shopify embedded checkout, and magic link authentication.
What AI models power the music and lyrics generation pipeline?
The system combines a two-stage LLM workflow: Gemini 2.5 Flash handles fast structural ideation and rhyme drafting, while Claude Sonnet refines nuance and poetic cadence. The final conditioned prompt feeds into the Suno V5.5 audio engine for studio-quality multi-instrumental arrangement and vocal rendering.
How does the platform handle long-running generative audio requests?
Because audio synthesis requires 30–60 seconds, the backend decouples generation into asynchronous job queues. The client receives an instant task ID, and background workers listen for API webhooks. Once rendering is complete, real-time SSE updates notify the frontend, eliminating connection drops.
Can this architecture be adapted for other generative AI media products?
Yes. The decoupling of synchronous user sessions from long-running background generative tasks, combined with webhook-driven payment ledgers, applies directly to AI video synthesis, podcast generation, automated avatar production, and large-scale marketing asset creation.
