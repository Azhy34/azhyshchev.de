AI Chatbot & Customer Support Automation: Vectorize Any Data Source for RAG Agents in Minutes

How companies turn websites, PDF catalogs, and databases into instant vector search engines using Google ADK and serverless Cloud Run.

Never Waste Paid Ad Traffic Again (Zero Lost Leads)

Every click from Google Ads or LinkedIn Ads costs money — often $10 to $50+ per B2B click. When an interested prospect reaches out after hours (e.g. at 7:00 PM) and gets a reply the next morning, your ad spend is wasted: the prospect has already booked a call with a faster competitor.

🛡️ The B2B Sales ROI:

A RAG AI Sales Agent protects your marketing budget. It responds in sub-second latency, answers client questions from your indexed data, and books sales calls directly into your calendar 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Fast Vectorization: Turn Any Data Source Into Instant Knowledge

Building an AI assistant no longer requires long, expensive integration projects. Modern vector search pipelines allow you to index any knowledge source in minutes:

  • Websites & Sitemaps: Real-time vector indexing of your public domain via Vertex AI Search.
  • PDF Documents & Catalogs: Technical specification sheets, user guides, and price lists indexed in minutes.
  • Databases & Product Catalogs: Live data integration for stock levels and factual pricing.
